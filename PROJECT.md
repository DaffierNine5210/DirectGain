# Vision

Direct Gain is a trust-first marketplace and community platform that combines buying, selling, jobs, live auctions and messaging into one experience.

Our goal is to make online trading and networking feel safer, more transparent and more enjoyable by putting reputation at the centre of every interaction.

# Gain Score™

Gain Score is Direct Gain's reputation system.

It represents a user's trustworthiness across the platform.

Gain Score cannot be purchased.

It is earned.
