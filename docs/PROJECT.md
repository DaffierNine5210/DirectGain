# Direct Gain

**Slogan:** Grow Together

Direct Gain is a trust-first local opportunity platform that brings buying, selling, work, live auctions, messaging, community activity, and professional reputation into one app.

The product is being built for iOS and Android using React Native, Expo, and TypeScript.

---

## 1. Product Vision

Direct Gain should become the place people open when they want to discover opportunities nearby.

Those opportunities may include:

- Items for sale
- Local jobs
- Professional services
- Live auctions
- Businesses
- Community activity
- People with useful skills
- Trusted local connections

The product should feel enjoyable to browse while remaining practical, secure, and locally relevant.

---

## 2. Mission

Help people grow by making it easier to:

- Buy and sell locally
- Find work
- Offer services
- Build a trusted reputation
- Connect directly
- Participate in live auctions
- Discover useful opportunities nearby

Direct Gain should make local economic activity more accessible and trustworthy.

---

## 3. Core Product Principles

### Trust First

Trust is more important than follower counts.

Profiles, listings, jobs, auctions, and messages should surface useful trust information such as:

- Identity verification
- Business verification
- Professional verification
- Community reputation
- Ratings and reviews
- Completed transactions
- Completed jobs
- Auction history
- Gain Score

### Local Relevance

Users should see opportunities that are relevant to their location and selected distance range.

Location sharing must be privacy-conscious and user controlled.

### One Connected Ecosystem

Market, jobs, auctions, messages, profiles, community activity, and payments should feel like connected parts of one product.

### Free Core Experience

Everyday users should be able to browse, buy, sell, find work, message, join auctions, and participate in the community without mandatory subscription fees.

### Premium Without Clutter

The interface should feel modern and polished without excessive glow, animation, advertising, or visual noise.

### Build for Reuse

Shared components, theme tokens, navigation patterns, and data structures should be reused instead of recreated screen by screen.

## 6. Gain Score

Gain Score is Direct Gain's reputation system.

It may consider:

- Verified identity
- Ratings and reviews
- Successful transactions
- Completed jobs
- Auction history
- Response time
- Disputes
- Reports
- Community behaviour
- Profile completeness

Gain Score should appear where trust matters, including:

- Profiles
- Market listings
- Job posts
- Auctions
- Messaging trust previews

The exact scoring model will be designed and tested before production launch.

---

## 7. Verification and Badges

Planned verification types include:

### Identity Verified

Green shield with a white checkmark.

### Business Verified

Black shield with a green briefcase.

### Professional Verified

Gold professional or trade badge.

### Community Trusted

Black-and-gold trust badge earned through strong platform history.

Badges must remain visually distinct and should never create confusion about what has been verified.

---

## 8. Monetisation

Direct Gain should remain free for normal everyday use.

Optional revenue sources may include:

- Featured listings
- Business subscriptions
- Boosted job posts
- Secure payment services
- Premium memberships
- Local advertising
- Delivery partnerships
- Business analytics
- Enterprise tools

Advertising must not interrupt core browsing, messaging, transactions, jobs, or auctions.
